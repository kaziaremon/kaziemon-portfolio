A production-ready, royal purple dark-mode 3D storytelling personal portfolio website built for **Kazi Emon** (Md Kazi Abdur Rahim Emon), highlighting:
- **Facebook Marketing and Ads**
- **Instagram Marketing and Ads**
- **Social Media Managing**
- **Google Ads**
- **Canva Poster Design**
- **Strategic Business Planning** (incorporating an Accounting & financial perspective)

Live Custom Domain: [kaziemon.online](https://kaziemon.online/)

---

## 🚀 Key Architectural Features

1. **Royal Purple Dark-Mode Aesthetic**:
   - Deep rich purples (`#0c0418`, `#1a0b2e`, `#240e3f`, `#2d124f`), glowing violet accents (`#a855f7`, `#c084fc`), and high-contrast typography (`#ffffff` and `#e2d9f3`).
   - Interactive Three.js 3D WebGL particle wave in royal purple and violet hues.
   - 3D card tilt interactions with specular glow on hover.

2. **Section Architecture (7 Exact Links)**:
   - **Home**: Oversized typography **"I am Kazi Emon"**, positioning 2 years of experience and 12+ projects delivered (strictly no photo in Hero).
   - **About**: Professional author bio detailing how an **Accounting student** background brings financial acumen, unit economics, and ROI discipline to marketing, accompanied by a matching portrait photograph with a glowing purple frame.
   - **Service**: 6 core service cards with interactive "Read More" modal deep-dives.
   - **Blueprint**: 4-phase execution framework (Discovery & Strategy, Creative & Asset Development with Canva, Campaign Deployment, Optimization & Scale).
   - **Article**: 3 human-written strategy articles rendered from a scalable modular dataset (`js/articles.js`) with full reader modal views.
   - **FAQ**: 4 realistic client Q&A items covering campaign structures, Canva design, business planning with accounting acumen, and onboarding.
   - **Contact**: Direct WhatsApp (`01560066374`), Email (`info@kaziemon.online`), LinkedIn, Facebook, and a prominent **"Send a Message"** direct mailto action.

3. **Strict Content Purge**:
   - Zero traces of server-side tracking, CAPI, GTM server containers, ad simulators, ROI calculators, engagement-tracking widgets, budgeting tables, or currency mentions.
