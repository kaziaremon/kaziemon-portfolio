Professional Journey

Where Financial Acumen Meets Digital Execution

My name is Md Kazi Abdur Rahim Emon (known professionally as Kazi Emon). Over the past two years, I have established myself as an independent digital marketing specialist and growth strategist based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, delivering 12+ successful client projects across retail, service, and online brand verticals.

What truly differentiates my work in a crowded marketing industry is my foundational background as an Accounting student. While traditional marketers often treat campaigns purely as creative exercises—celebrating superficial metrics like "impressions" or "likes"—my accounting perspective demands that every campaign be grounded in real-world commercial viability.

In business, capital allocation is sacred. Because I study financial statements, cost of goods sold (COGS), gross contribution margins, and cash conversion cycles, I approach your promotional initiatives like an investment portfolio. We don’t just buy clicks; we protect your margins, evaluate customer acquisition costs against lifetime value, and construct marketing funnels designed to produce genuine net profit.

Whether crafting high-contrast promotional posters on Canva, engineering targeted ad architectures on Meta and Google, or consulting on strategic business roadmaps, my focus remains constant: disciplined execution, transparent communication, and measurable growth.

Execution Roadmap

The 4-Phase Growth Blueprint

A structured, repeatable methodology showing exactly how client campaigns are built from raw vision to scalable commercial success.

Phase 01

Discovery & Strategy

We align your primary commercial objectives with detailed market research. Incorporating strategic business planning insights and unit economic analysis, we define your core value proposition and profile your highest-converting audience segments.

Phase 02

Creative & Asset Development

We engineer high-impact direct-response ad copy and craft professional visual promotional assets using Canva. Every graphic is styled with strict brand color discipline, legible typography, and compelling hooks tailored to stop social feed scrolling.

Phase 03

Campaign Deployment

We build and launch structured advertising pipelines across Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google Ads. Campaigns are segmented by intent levels—from cold audience discovery to warm consideration and high-urgency promotional offers.

Phase 04

Optimization & Scale

We continuously evaluate key performance metrics, eliminating underperforming creative angles and reallocating capital to winning audiences. Through systematic fine-targeting and iterative adjustments, we maximize client return on investment.
