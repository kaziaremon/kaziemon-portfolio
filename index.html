My name is Md Kazi Abdur Rahim Emon (known professionally as Kazi Emon). Over the past 2 years, I have worked as a dedicated digital marketing specialist in Dhaka, Bangladesh, successfully executing 12+ client projects across retail, service, and modern digital brands.

While many marketers focus solely on vanity metrics like clicks and impressions, my accounting foundation compels me to evaluate campaigns through the lens of true commercial profitability—analyzing gross margins, cost per acquisition, customer lifetime value, and cash flow sustainability.

Whether engineering targeted ad pipelines on Meta and Google, designing eye-catching promotional posters in Canva, or formulating 90-day business growth roadmaps, my mission is simple: eliminate wasted spend, protect your margins, and deliver measurable return on investment.

Execution Roadmap

The 4-Phase Growth Blueprint

A structured, repeatable methodology showing exactly how client campaigns are built from raw vision to scalable commercial success.

Phase 01

Discovery & Strategy

We align your primary commercial objectives with detailed market research. Incorporating strategic business planning insights and unit economic analysis, we define your core value proposition and profile your highest-converting audience segments.

Phase 02

Creative & Asset Development

We engineer high-impact direct-response ad copy and craft professional visual promotional assets using Canva. Every graphic is styled with strict brand color discipline, legible typography, and compelling hooks tailored to stop social feed scrolling.

Phase 03

Campaign Deployment

We build and launch structured advertising pipelines across Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google Ads. Campaigns are segmented by intent levels—from cold audience discovery to warm consideration and high-urgency promotional offers.

Phase 04

Optimization & Scale

We continuously evaluate key performance metrics, eliminating underperforming creative angles and reallocating capital to winning audiences. Through systematic fine-targeting and iterative adjustments, we maximize client return on investment.
